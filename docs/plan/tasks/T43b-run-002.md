## Rules (protocol v3, tagged before run-002)
1. **Cells cut by tile edges:** 5 km cells are squares of one global equal-area grid (EASE-Grid 2.0, EPSG:6933), not of each tile's local projection. A cell that straddles two 1° tiles is one cell with the windows of both. It is assessed by the tile that holds its centre, which also reads the windows of its neighbours.
2. **Candidates on reliable cells only:** a candidate cell must have high confidence. Zones are made of, and ranked on, high-confidence cells only.

Unchanged: the windows (already computed), the masks, τ, the minimum zone size, the controls and the pass thresholds.
